# Achievement Atlas

This is a tactical checklist. Verify exact in-game menus because WorldBox versions can change.

## Core rule

Some achievements require units without trait-editor intervention. If a unit has Scar of Divinity / Divine Scar, treat it as disqualified for strict checks.

## Low-risk quick wins

### The Princess

Recipe:

1. Spawn frog.
2. Use Blessing on frog.
3. Inspect/verify achievement.

No-mod mode:

- one tool selection
- one click
- screenshot verify

### Super Mushroom

Recipe:

1. Spawn creature.
2. Use MUSH Spores.
3. Kill creature so it converts into Mush.
4. Use Powerup on Mush.
5. Verify.

### The Demon

Recipe:

1. Spawn Demon.
2. Use MUSH Spores.
3. Use Zombie Infection.
4. Use Madness.
5. Open/view Demon character menu.

### Ninja Turtle

Recipe:

1. Spawn turtle.
2. Use Madness on turtle.
3. Spawn grasshoppers in controlled batches.
4. Turtle must reach level 10.
5. Use sonic speed/world-law toggles only when safe.

Avoid grasshopper spam in no-mod mode. Use small batches and verify leveling.

### Ant World

Recipe:

1. Spawn 10 Blue Ants.
2. Spawn 10 Black Ants.
3. Spawn 10 Green Ants.
4. Spawn 10 Red Ants.
5. Verify.

## Medium/high-risk farms

### The Accomplished

Requires unit with:

- Veteran
- Mageslayer
- Dragonslayer
- Kingslayer
- no trait editor

High-level route:

1. Prepare fresh unit.
2. Weaken Evil Mage safely.
3. Let target kill Evil Mage.
4. Weaken Dragon safely.
5. Let target kill Dragon.
6. Isolate/weaken King.
7. Let target kill King.
8. Farm kills until Veteran.
9. Inspect target.

### King of Kings

Requires king with 20+ traits without trait editor.

Strict rules:

- king must stay alive
- do not give Madness to king because it can demote them
- heir/king must not have Scar of Divinity
- Blood Rain/healing ready
- use controlled arena only

## Collection achievements

General unlock sweep:

- inspect creatures and hover/tap new traits
- inspect subspecies traits
- inspect culture traits
- inspect language traits
- inspect clan traits
- inspect religions
- inspect equipment
- open relevant menus to trigger achievements
