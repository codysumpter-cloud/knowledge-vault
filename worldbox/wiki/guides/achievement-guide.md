# Achievement Guide: The Completionist Atlas

Tactical recipes for WorldBox achievements. 

## Strict Requirement
Any unit with **Scar of Divinity** or **Divine Scar** is disqualified from strict trait checks.

## Low-Risk Recipes
### The Princess
- **Recipe:** Spawn frog $\rightarrow$ use Blessing.
- **Verification:** Screenshot check.

### Super Mushroom
- **Recipe:** Spawn creature $\rightarrow$ MUSH Spores $\rightarrow$ Kill $\rightarrow$ Powerup on resulting Mush.

### The Demon
- **Recipe:** Spawn Demon $\rightarrow$ MUSH Spores $\rightarrow$ Zombie Infection $\rightarrow$ Madness.

### Ninja Turtle
- **Recipe:** Spawn turtle $\rightarrow$ Madness $\rightarrow$ Farm grasshoppers to Level 10.

## High-Risk Farms
### The Accomplished
Requires a unit with Veteran, Mageslayer, Dragonslayer, and Kingslayer.
- **Strategy:** Weaken target (Evil Mage/Dragon/King) safely, let the target unit deliver the final blow.

### King of Kings
King with 20+ traits.
- **Constraint:** Do not use Madness on the king (prevents demotion).
- **Safety:** Use Blood Rain and healing in a controlled arena.
