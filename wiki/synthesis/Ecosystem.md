🌌 The Prismtek Ecosystem
Prismtek is a converged creative and technical hub. It's not just a site (prismtek.dev), but a synergy of:
- The Creative Surface: Pixel Studio, browser arcade games, and community spaces.
- The Game Lab: High-concept deterministic projects like Wildlands Critter Clash (Bible-driven pipeline), WorldBox, and Pokemon Champions.
- The Agentic Core: A sophisticated infrastructure for autonomous AI (The BMO Stack, Agentic-Stack, and Hermes).
