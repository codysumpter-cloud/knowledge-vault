# Market Thesis Workflow

## Purpose

Create a disciplined prediction-market thesis.

## Workflow

```txt
1. Capture market title and prices.
2. Read resolution rules.
3. Convert price to implied probability.
4. List evidence for YES.
5. List evidence for NO.
6. Identify catalysts.
7. Identify what would change the view.
8. Check liquidity/spread.
9. Write no-action case.
10. Produce manual-only checklist.
```

## Price interpretation

Prediction-market prices can be treated as rough implied probabilities before fees, spreads, liquidity, and market microstructure.

A 63¢ YES price roughly means the market is pricing YES near 63%, but execution and exit may differ.

## Required caveat

A market can be directionally right and still be a bad trade if:

- spread is wide
- liquidity is poor
- resolution rules are ambiguous
- catalyst timing is uncertain
- fees/slippage erase the edge
- the user's thesis is not independent from the market price

## Required warning

This is decision support, not a guarantee or instruction to wager/trade. Only risk money you can afford to lose, and confirm legality and platform eligibility in your jurisdiction.
