# Market Adapter Contract

Supports sportsbook and prediction-market analysis.

## Allowed without approval

- educational explanations
- implied probability calculations
- odds conversion
- bankroll risk simulations
- thesis generation
- risk checklist
- market monitoring summaries
- post-mortems

## Always blocked from auto-execution

- place bet
- place trade
- deposit funds
- withdraw funds
- transfer funds
- change leverage/exposure
- open account
- bypass location/jurisdiction checks

## Required warning

Every output that discusses a bet, wager, trade, or market position must include:

- "This is decision support, not a guarantee or instruction to wager/trade."
- "Only risk money you can afford to lose."
- "Confirm legality and platform eligibility in your jurisdiction."

## Manual-only default

The skill may prepare a decision note.

The user must manually make any final betting or trading decision outside Buddy/Hermes.
