# Risk and Resolution Review

## Purpose

Prevent bad prediction-market decisions caused by misunderstood rules, weak liquidity, or unclear exits.

## Checklist

```txt
Resolution source:
Exact wording:
End date:
Ambiguous terms:
Market price:
Spread:
Liquidity:
Max loss:
Exit plan:
Catalysts:
Reasons to do nothing:
```

## Red flags

- unclear resolution source
- subjective wording
- thin liquidity
- wide spread
- user cannot define max loss
- user wants to chase prior losses
- market depends on inaccessible insider information
- market depends on illegal or harmful activity

## Safer alternatives

- watchlist only
- paper trade
- write a thesis without entering
- wait for better liquidity
- wait for clearer resolution
- reduce exposure
- do nothing

## Stop signal

If the user says they are desperate, need guaranteed income, are chasing losses, or cannot afford the downside:

```txt
I can’t help optimize a trade in that situation. The safer move is not to trade. I can help you step back, write a risk note, or make a non-trading plan.
```
