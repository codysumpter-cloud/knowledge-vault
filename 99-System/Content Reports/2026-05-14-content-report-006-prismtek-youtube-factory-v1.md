## Policy Posture

The factory explicitly avoids:

- passive-income framing
- money-printer framing
- unverified income claims
- copied source-post language
- mass-produced repetitive templates
- reused/scraped content as the main product
- undisclosed realistic synthetic/altered content
- secret-bearing receipts

## Upload Boundary

The workflow supports manual-assisted YouTube Studio upload, but does not auto-publish. Publishing and scheduling remain approval-gated.

Approval phrase:

```txt
GO YOUTUBE <video-slug>
```
